# Art direction and asset plan

Audience: environment and vehicle artists, technical artists and production. Purpose: establish a coherent visual language and asset acceptance process. Status: draft; appearance and period fit are not yet approved in a shared scene.

## Visual target

Use the supplied Panzer IV as the starting reference: a stylized low-poly vehicle with distinct hull, turret and gun assets and a flat-color texture atlas. Aim for clear silhouettes, restrained earth tones, readable equipment and consistent lighting. Low-poly refers to presentation, not simplified or unsupported claims about armor, handling or the tank's history. Damage and combat events should be visible in a replay without obscuring essential information.

The archive has FBX and DAE components; an initial FBX import did not automatically assemble or texture the model. Its license is not established by receiving the file. Confirm reuse, modification and redistribution terms before a distributable release. See the [asset inventory](../../assets/README.md) for downloaded files, sources and hashes.

## Asset pipeline

Record original creator, source URL, version, license text and attribution, archive hash, acquisition date, derived files and modifications. Preserve original archives. Import with a consistent physical scale, axes, pivots and materials; normalize turret/gun articulation, ground contact and collision geometry. Record conversions so replay versions can request the exact content bundle. Never infer reuse rights from a free download or use ripped assets. Keep unverified assets out of distributable builds.

Build a comparison scene containing the assembled Panzer IV, other candidate tanks and representative trees, roads, buildings and debris under the same camera and lighting. Inspect silhouette, palette, texture sharpness, scale and materials at both spectator and sight distances. Capture actual rendered evidence for art review; reference images are targets, not proof. Review period-specific details with a researcher and check mobile/desktop browser performance before approval.

## Acceptance

All first-release categories have acquired, rights-reviewed assets and derived formats; vehicles assemble correctly with working pivots and texture; scene scale, palette and visual readability are approved from real captures; agent sights use the same world geometry; historical review and runtime performance are recorded. Coverage of all WWII tanks requires further sourcing and original content work and is not currently satisfied.
