## User-favored tank reference

[Lowpoly Tank Pack 01](https://isolatedsoulstudio.itch.io/lowpoly-tank-pack-01) by IsolatedSoulStudio: the creator lists IS-2, KV-2, Panzer IV, M4 Sherman, Tiger II and T-34-76, each with separated body, turret and barrel; FBX and DAE, 1024-pixel color map. The Panzer IV is a free sample; the complete pack requires at least $5. The page does not state reuse or redistribution terms; a commenter asks about the license without a visible answer. Treat as a visual reference only until its rights are verified. It does not establish interiors, detailed controls, historical accuracy, or full roster coverage. The page metadata also names IS-3 rather than IS-2, so the paid pack contents need direct verification. Accessed 2026-11-02 UTC.
